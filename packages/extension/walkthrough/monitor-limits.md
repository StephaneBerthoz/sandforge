## Monitor org limits

What an org has left, before you hit a wall:

- API limits, active jobs, and storage, with a health score per org.
- Read when you open Monitor or press **Refresh**; **Auto-refresh** reads again every 30 s while the page is open, and each read checks the alert thresholds. Nothing is watched while Monitor is closed.

[Open Monitor](command:sandforge.openMonitor)
