## Tune your settings

Make SandForge fit your workflow:

- Default batch sizes for Seed and Sync operations.
- Production Guard, on by default, and the record of its decisions -- kept with each run in the audit trail (Reports), unless you turn it off.
- Optional AI assistant, powered by Anthropic (Claude).
- How Forge shows a large graph: drawn up to 25 objects and listed in a table past that, or always one way (`sandforge.forge.graphView`).

[Open Settings](command:sandforge.openSettings)
